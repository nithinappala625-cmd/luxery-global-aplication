Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding private charter coordination, operational procedures, and booking terms.

Private flight quotations are determined by flight duration, aircraft hourly rates, positioning or ferry sectors, landing fees, airport parking, ground handling charges, and applicable crew overnight expenses. Our desk provides comprehensive quotations detailing all inclusions upfront.
For domestic sectors with aircraft readily positioned, flight arrangements can often be coordinated within 2 to 4 hours, subject to crew duty times, aircraft availability, and airport slot clearances. International flights typically require 24 to 48 hours for overflight and landing permits.
Baggage capacity varies significantly by aircraft category. Light jets and helicopters accommodate soft-sided duffel bags, while Super-Midsize and Ultra-Long-Range business jets feature spacious pressurized, accessible baggage compartments suitable for standard hard-shell luggage and sports equipment.
Private charters require advance settlement prior to departure via wire transfer or designated corporate payment methods. Cancellation terms are defined in accordance with the operating carrier's policy and are explicitly communicated prior to booking confirmation.
All passengers must present government-issued photo identification (such as an Aadhaar card or Passport for domestic travel within India, or valid Passports with required visas for international sectors) for manifest generation and security clearances prior to boarding.
Royendison Aviation operates as an independent air charter broker and client representative. We do not own or directly operate aircraft. All flights are conducted by certified DGCA, FAA, or EASA Part 135 air operators who maintain full operational control and safety responsibility.
